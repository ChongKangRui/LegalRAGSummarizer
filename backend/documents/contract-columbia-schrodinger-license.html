RECITALS
A.    Since 1994, Columbia and Schrödinger have entered into various license agreements pursuant to which Columbia has licensed to Schrödinger the right (i) to incorporate the technology underlying various Columbia-owned intellectual property into Schrödinger’s proprietary software and (ii) to exploit Schrödinger’s proprietary software, including to perform services for the benefit of Third Parties.
